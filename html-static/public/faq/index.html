Frequently asked questions

Here are answers to the questions we hear most often at the counter. If you cannot find what you are looking for, please get in touch and a member of our team will be glad to help.

Do I need to pre-order bread?

No, most days we have plenty of bread on the shelves until early afternoon. If you need a specific loaf or a large quantity, call us before 9 am and we will set it aside for you.

Do you offer vegan options?

Yes. All of our sourdough breads are vegan, as they contain only flour, water, salt and our wild yeast starter. Our pastries contain butter and eggs, so they are not suitable for a vegan diet.

Can you make gluten-free bread?

Unfortunately not. Wheat flour is used throughout our kitchen, so we cannot prevent cross-contact and do not sell any products labelled as gluten-free.

Do you cater events and offices?

We do. We supply pastry platters, sandwich loaves and breakfast boxes for groups of ten or more. Please give us at least 48 hours notice for catering orders.

What happens to bread you do not sell?

Every evening we donate unsold bread to a local food bank, and anything left over is turned into breadcrumbs or croutons for the next day.

How should I store my sourdough?

Keep it cut side down on a wooden board or in a linen bag for up to three days. For longer storage, slice it and freeze it, then toast slices straight from the freezer.
