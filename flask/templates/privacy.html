Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Bookworm Bookstore takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, shop with us or sign up for our newsletter, how we use that information and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you send us a message or place a special order we collect your name, email address and any details you include in your message. If you join our loyalty scheme we store your name, email address and a record of your stamps. Our website collects basic, anonymous usage statistics such as the pages visited and the type of browser used. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use your information

We use your details only to answer your enquiries, fulfil orders, run the loyalty scheme and, if you have opted in, send our monthly newsletter. We never sell or rent customer information. Payment card details are processed by our card terminal provider and are never stored by us.

Keeping your data safe

Customer records are stored on secure, access-controlled systems and are kept only as long as needed. Enquiries are deleted after twelve months, and newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
