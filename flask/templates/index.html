More than a bookshop

We are a proudly independent, family-run shop with a team of seven booksellers who read widely and love to talk about books. Our shelves are hand-picked rather than chosen by algorithm, so you will find small-press gems and local authors alongside the bestsellers. If we do not have what you are looking for, we can order almost any book in print within a few days at no extra cost.

Every month we host author readings, signings and book club evenings, and every Saturday morning our children’s room fills up for story time. Upstairs you will find a quiet reading room with armchairs and a wood stove, and our café serves locally roasted coffee and pastries from the bakery next door.

  • New, used and signed books across every genre
  • Free special orders and flat-rate US shipping
  • Monthly author events and book clubs
  • Gift cards that never expire
