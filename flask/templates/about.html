Our story

Bookworm Bookstore opened in 1998 in a former hardware store on Elm Street, with a few hundred second-hand books and a borrowed cash register. Founder Ruth Whitaker had spent twenty years as a school librarian and wanted to create the kind of shop she had always wished existed: welcoming to everyone, unhurried, and run by people who genuinely love books.

Today the shop is run by Ruth’s son Dan and a small team of booksellers. We have grown to fill both floors of the building, added a children’s room and a reading café, and now stock around twenty thousand new and used titles. The original cash register still sits on the counter, although these days it is mostly decorative.

What we believe

We believe a good bookshop is a public living room. You are welcome to browse for as long as you like, read in the armchairs upstairs, or simply come in for a chat about what you are reading. We choose every book on our shelves by hand, give generous space to local and independent publishers, and pay fair prices for the second-hand books our customers bring in.

We also give back to the community that has supported us for over twenty-five years. Each year we donate books to Maplewood’s schools and library, host free literacy workshops for adults, and run a summer reading challenge that encourages hundreds of local children to keep reading during the holidays.
